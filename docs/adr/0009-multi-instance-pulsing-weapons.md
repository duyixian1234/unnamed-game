# Multi-instance melee and pulsing orb weapons

Version 0.5.0 expands the former single-slot MVP loadout to up to six independent Weapon Slots. Same-kind weapons share their WeaponKind level and upgrade stats, while each instance keeps its own attack timing and damage contribution; melee uses a nearest-target 120-degree fan, and orbiting orbs pulse from the Player to maximum radius and back over a fixed 1.2-second cycle. The independent instances preserve close-range coverage as weapons are added without making orb upgrades permanently abandon the Player.

2026-08 amendment: independent-instance damage applies to the *unevolved* state only. At the MeleeSwing Level-6 Evolution (the behavior-change exception in ADR-0008) all MeleeSwing instances merge into one Whirlwind on the lowest occupied slot, pooling their damage into a single blade — the per-instance whirlwinds were position-identical, so separate slots granted no coverage the merged blade lacks. Multi-instance melee timing also staggers: instances are phase-offset so they never swing on the same frame. Orbiting-orb instances alternate spin direction (clockwise / counter-clockwise by global ordinal) and tint.
