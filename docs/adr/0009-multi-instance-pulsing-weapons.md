# Multi-instance melee and pulsing orb weapons

Version 0.5.0 expands the former single-slot MVP loadout to up to six independent Weapon Slots. Same-kind weapons share their WeaponKind level and upgrade stats, while each instance keeps its own attack timing and damage contribution; melee uses a nearest-target 120-degree fan, and orbiting orbs pulse from the Player to maximum radius and back over a fixed 1.2-second cycle. The independent instances preserve close-range coverage as weapons are added without making orb upgrades permanently abandon the Player.
